## An Alternative: Select Responses, Then Imitate

**Rejection-sampling finetuning** keeps high-scoring responses and trains on them with ordinary cross-entropy.

1. Sample several responses per prompt.
2. Score them and keep the best, or those above a threshold.
3. Finetune on the selected responses.

Rejected responses contribute **no direct loss term**. With signed advantages, policy gradient also learns from worse-than-baseline attempts.

This can use one fixed generated dataset or repeat collection and finetuning. Inference-time **Best-of-N** selects an answer without updating weights.

## The DPO Insight

Rafailov et al. (2023): the KL-regularized RL objective from RLHF has a **closed-form optimal policy**.

:::columns cols="2" gap="34px"
- Rearranged, the **reward becomes implicit** in the policy's own log-probabilities relative to the reference
+++
- So: no separate **reward model**, no **RL loop**
:::

The reward model and the policy were two views of the same thing. DPO optimizes the policy **directly** from preferences. <!-- .element: class="text-lg" style="margin-top: 12px;" -->

---

<!-- .slide: id="dpo-loss" -->

## The DPO Loss

One **supervised classification loss** on chosen-vs-rejected pairs, against a frozen reference:

$$\mathcal L_{\text{DPO}} = -\mathbb E_{(x, y_w, y_l)}\Big[\log \sigma\Big(\textcolor{#4a9eff}{\beta \log \tfrac{\pi_\theta(y_w \mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_w \mid x)}} - \textcolor{#f5a623}{\beta \log \tfrac{\pi_\theta(y_l \mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_l \mid x)}}\Big)\Big]$$

:::columns cols="2" gap="34px"
- Raise the policy's probability of the **winner** $\textcolor{#4a9eff}{y_w}$ relative to the reference
- Lower it for the **loser** $\textcolor{#f5a623}{y_l}$
+++
- $\beta$ controls how far the policy may move from the reference
- No sampling, no reward model, no PPO &mdash; just **SFT-style** gradient descent
:::

## Why DPO Mattered

:::columns cols="2" gap="34px"
**What it removed**

- No sampling loop
- No reward model
- No PPO instability
- Runs on the **same machinery as SFT**
+++
**The impact**

- Preference tuning became **reproducible and accessible**
- The workhorse behind many open-weight models
:::

Standard DPO learns from a **fixed preference dataset**. It does not collect fresh attempts in its update loop, although the trained model can generalize beyond those examples. <!-- .element: class="text-lg" style="margin-top: 12px;" -->
