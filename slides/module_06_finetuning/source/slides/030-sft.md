## The Data Unit Changes

Pretraining trained on raw text. SFT trains on **prompt&ndash;response pairs**.

:::columns cols="2" gap="34px"
**Pretraining example**

```text
...to be, or not to be, that is
the question: whether 'tis nobler...
```
A continuous stream. The target: the next character.
+++
**SFT example**

```text
prompt:   uppercase: hello
response: HELLO
```
A demonstration of the behavior we want.
:::

Collect many demonstrations. Train the model to **produce the response given the prompt**. <!-- .element: class="text-lg" style="margin-top: 12px;" -->

## Instruction Tuning Generalizes

Train on **many tasks phrased as instructions** and the model learns **instruction-following itself**. It transfers to instructions it never saw.

:::columns cols="3" gap="22px"
**FLAN**

- Wei et al., 2021
- 60+ tasks
- Strong **zero-shot** generalization to held-out tasks
+++
**T0**

- Sanh et al., 2021
- Multitask prompted training
- Zero-shot task generalization
+++
**Super-NaturalInstructions**

- 1,600+ tasks
- Task diversity drives generalization
:::

You are not teaching every task. You are teaching **what it means to follow an instruction**. <!-- .element: class="text-lg" style="margin-top: 12px;" -->
